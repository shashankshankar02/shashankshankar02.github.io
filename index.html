How I diagnose a ROAS drop

In this order
  1. Impression shareAre we losing auctions to rank or to budget? The fixes are different.
  2. BiddingIs the tROAS target realistic for what the campaign delivers, or is it throttling volume?
  3. Channel mixOn Performance Max, is spend drifting into Display, Gmail or Discover placements?
  4. ProductsBest sellers out of stock, Merchant Center disapprovals, feed issues.
  5. TrackingCompare Google Ads with Shopify and GA4. A broken tag looks exactly like a ROAS crash.

Only then do I change bids or budgets.
